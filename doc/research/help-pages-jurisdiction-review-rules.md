# Internal review, deadlines and external reviewers by jurisdiction

Research for issue #1140, which feeds #626, #625 and #698 under map #1138.
All legislation was fetched on 2026-10-22 from the official legislation site for each jurisdiction.
Source keys such as [F1] are listed under "Sources fetched" at the end, with the exact URL fetched.
This note records findings only and makes no recommendation about help page copy.

## Question

For the federal government and all eight states and territories, does the FOI Act provide internal review, what is the response deadline and how are its days counted, and who is the external reviewer?

## Findings table

## Disagreements between the repo and the primary sources

The repo files compared are `lib/model_patches.rb` (`PublicBody#reply_late_after_days`, `#working_or_calendar_days`, `#legislation`) and the Jurisdictions section of `README.md`.
The comparison was made against branch `worktree-agent-a385aa03805e952d1` at commit e987237.

Comparison with the primary sources.

1. ACT: the code uses 30 calendar days. The Act says 30 working days (FOI Act 2016 s 40(1)). The day count disagrees. [A1]
2. Victoria: the code uses 45 days. The Act says 30 days (FOI Act 1982 s 21(1)(a), version 115 in force from 1 September 2026). The number disagrees. [V1]
3. Northern Territory: the code maps the NT to the `foi` Legislation record, whose display strings are "Freedom of Information Act" and "FOI". The NT statute is the Information Act 2002, and it has no separate FOI Act. The deadline and day count agree (30 days, s 19(1)). Only the legislation name disagrees. [NT1]
4. Federal, NSW, Queensland, South Australia, Tasmania and Western Australia: the deadline, the day counting basis and the legislation key agree with the Acts. Queensland and Tasmania are correctly mapped to the Right to Information Acts, and NSW to the GIPA Act. [F1] [N1] [Q1] [S1] [T1] [W1]
5. `README.md`: the Jurisdictions section lists tags only. It states no deadlines, day counting or legislation names, so there is nothing in it to compare against.
6. `AGENTS.md` refers to `PublicBody#external_reviewer`, but that method is not in `lib/model_patches.rb` at the commit compared. The codebase index shows it on another checkout. It was not compared, because the branch checked out here does not contain it.
7. The code does not model extensions, clock stops or the 20 and 30 day review windows. These are differences in scope, not disagreements, so they are not counted above.

## Unverified or not fully checked

- The meaning of "days" in the federal, Victorian, South Australian, Western Australian and Northern Territory Acts. Each says "days" with no working day qualifier. Reading that as calendar days relies on the absence of a qualifier. The interpretation Acts that define "day" were not fetched.
- The definitions of "working day" or "business day" for NSW, Tasmania, Queensland and the ACT were not fetched. In particular, which public holidays count was not checked. The Queensland term is "business days" and the code labels it "working".
- The host Alaveteli code that turns `working` into a date (`Holiday.due_date_from`) and its holiday data were not examined.
- Extension rules, clock-stopping rules and the point at which the period starts were read only where they are cited in the table. They were not checked in full for any jurisdiction.
- Queensland Information Privacy Act 2009 s 164A (response period for privacy complaints) was identified from the contents list and not read in full.
- The NSW Information and Privacy Commission fact sheet on external review was seen only as a headline on the IPC home page. The reviewer for NSW rests on GIPA Act s 89. The statute is the basis for every reviewer named in the table. The reviewer websites were read to confirm each office exists and runs FOI review, not for further legal detail.
- Version dates were read for the federal (compilation 122, registered C2026C00433, in force from 29 September 2026), Victorian (version 115, 1 September 2026), Queensland (current as at 1 July 2026), Western Australian (07-r0-00, from 1 July 2026) and Northern Territory (as in force at 1 June 2026) texts. The NSW, SA and Tasmanian texts were fetched from "current" URLs without a version date being read. The ACT text fetched is republication 16, effective 16 December 2025, which the ACT site served as current.
- The federal legislation.gov.au page is a script-driven site, so the text was read from the Word file served by the Federal Register of Legislation API, not from the page.
- Responses served through Cloudflare (NSW and SA legislation, several reviewer sites) were fetched with a browser session. The Tasmanian legislation site truncates uncompressed responses, so the text was fetched with gzip. Neither affects the content read.
